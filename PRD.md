# HITFACT — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

## Vision

Build a credible, social-first independent digital media platform where
users can consume news, fact checks and explainers and participate
through comments, polls, quizzes and forms.

## Primary audiences

1.  General news audience
2.  Social-media users who want context
3.  Readers interested in political/media claims
4.  Researchers and contributors
5.  HITFACT editorial team

## Product principles

- Evidence over narrative
- Latest content is easy to discover
- Fact and opinion are clearly separated
- Interaction without sacrificing moderation
- Mobile first
- Fast and accessible
- Editorial independence
